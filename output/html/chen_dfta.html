Legal & Compliance Landscape

The Older Americans Act and City charter require Department for the Aging to have an Advisory Council. The Mayor appoints all members, who serve for three years and aren't paid for their work.

✓NYC Aging · Advisory Council — https://www.nyc.gov/site/dfta/about/advisory-council.page
Cultural Norms

2022: Department for the Aging stops using the acronym “DFTA” on its website and social media channels to describe the agency, replacing it with NYC Aging, so residents have a clearer understanding of who the agency serves. The agency also stops using the term “senior” because of the negative connotations the word has in society.

✓NYC Aging · History — https://www.nyc.gov/site/dfta/about/history.page
