Mission Statement

The Center for Family Support is committed to providing support and assistance to individuals with developmental and related disabilities, and to the family members who care for them.

✓Center for Family Support · Mission and Vision — https://www.cfsny.org/about-cfs/mission-and-vision/
Stated Goals

To achieve our mission, we strive to: Support individuals to live the lives they want Respect different backgrounds, individual choice and overall family needs Provide families with the support they need at all stages of life Involve individuals in their communities Deliver excellent, individualized support to all

✓Center for Family Support · Mission and Vision — https://www.cfsny.org/about-cfs/mission-and-vision/
Stated Goals

The Center for Family Support is recognized as effective in helping people with developmental and related disabilities achieve self-determination. We have a reputation for trust, caring and excellence among individuals and families, regulators, colleague agencies, and general public.

✓Center for Family Support · Mission and Vision — https://www.cfsny.org/about-cfs/mission-and-vision/
Problems Solved

Community Habilitation provides one-to-one training to people with intellectual/developmental disabilities to develop or enhance the skills needed to live more independently in their homes or in the community.

✓Center for Family Support · New York Programs and Services — https://www.cfsny.org/supports-services/new-york/
Users / Beneficiaries

Today, CFS staff provide assistance and support to about 1,000 individuals with developmental disabilities of all ages in their home and communities across New York and New Jersey.

✓Center for Family Support · Mission and Vision — https://www.cfsny.org/about-cfs/mission-and-vision/
Users / Beneficiaries

We support 169 people throughout New York City's five boroughs and Westchester County in various settings. Depending on wants and needs, individuals might live on their own with staff coming in a few hours a day (supportive housing); live independently with help getting set up and managing household bills (individual support services); or they might need 24-hour support, where they live in small homes with others or in their own apartment (supervised IRAs).

✓Center for Family Support · New York Programs and Services — https://www.cfsny.org/supports-services/new-york/

Founders

In 1954 a small group of parents founded The Center for Family Support to meet the special needs of individuals with developmental disabilities and their families. The original intention of the organization was to provide respite services to individuals in order to maintain their loved ones at home.

✓Center for Family Support · Mission and Vision — https://www.cfsny.org/about-cfs/mission-and-vision/
Founders

The organization was founded by a pioneer in the field, Irene Arnold, who headed CFS until 1972. During that time, she focused on breaking up large facilities and bringing support into small group homes and into the homes and communities of families.

✓Center for Family Support · Mission and Vision — https://www.cfsny.org/about-cfs/mission-and-vision/
Employees / Creators

Although CFS has different programs, positions, and opportunities, many of our staff start with us as Direct Support Professionals, or DSPs. DSPs assist individuals with developmental disabilities to improve their quality of life, fulfill dreams and reach their goals. They serve as strong advocates for individuals, encourage them to communicate their wants and needs, and support their integration into their communities and neighborhoods.

✓Center for Family Support · Why Work at CFS? — https://www.cfsny.org/careers/
Employees / Creators

Infrastructure Requirements

Legal & Compliance Landscape

The Home and Community-Based Services Waiver is the Medicaid program that provides services for adults and children with developmental disabilities in their own home and community.

✓NYS Office for People With Developmental Disabilities · Home and Community Based Services Waiver — https://opwdd.ny.gov/providers/home-and-community-based-services-waiver
Legal & Compliance Landscape

The latest amendment of the OPWDD 1915 (c) Comprehensive Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS) Waiver was approved by the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) effective April 1, 2025.

✓NYS Office for People With Developmental Disabilities · Home and Community Based Services Waiver — https://opwdd.ny.gov/providers/home-and-community-based-services-waiver
Industry Standards

A new rule was issued by the federal government in 2014 called the HCBS Settings Rule. It, for the first time, defines "community" for all Medicaid-funded HCBS programs. The Rule ensures that people receive services in HCBS settings that help them be integrated in the community.

✓Institute on Community Integration (UMN) · The HCBS Settings Rule: The Critical Role of DSPs in Implementation — https://publications.ici.umn.edu/frontline-initiative/15-1/DSP-Role-HCBS-Rule
