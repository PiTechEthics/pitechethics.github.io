Affected fellows: **MOCS (Hal), OTI (Isabel), DFTA (Jingruo), NYPD (Ulysse).** For these four, first-party agency mission text and key statutory/policy PDFs (NYC AI Action Plan, NYPD DAS Impact-and-Use Policy, OIG-NYPD May 2024 audit, MOCS Annual Report, HDM Standards/Concept Paper) had to come from search-engine excerpts or third-party reproductions. `council.nyc.gov` was reachable directly (Suvadip's packet is on solid ground).
